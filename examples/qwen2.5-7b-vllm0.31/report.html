Assumptions and limits

  • One replica was profiled; several replicas are modelled as identical copies behind the router shown above.
  • Arrivals are Poisson at a constant rate. Bursty traffic needs more headroom than this report shows.
  • Prefix caching, speculative decoding, LoRA switching and multi-model serving are not modelled.
  • Results outside the profiled batch sizes and prompt lengths are extrapolated.
  • The 10–90% ranges reflect profile noise only; they are not a guarantee of production behaviour.
